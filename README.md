## 📖 How to Use snap - Detailed Guide

### 🖱️ The Click Method

- Look for the snap icon in your system tray (bottom-right corner of your screen)
- Click the icon once
- In the small window that pops up, click the big "Snap Out" button
- You'll instantly leave the Google Meet tab

### ⌨️ The Keyboard Shortcut Method

- Default shortcut: **Ctrl + Shift + S**
- You can change this in the settings (we'll cover that below)
- Press the shortcut anytime while in a meeting to leave immediately

### ⚙️ Customize Your Experience

Right-click the snap icon and choose "Settings" to adjust:

- **Custom Hotkey**: Change the keyboard shortcut to something that feels natural to you
- **Auto-Close Tab**: Choose whether snap closes the entire browser tab or just leaves the meeting
- **Start with Windows**: Make snap launch automatically when you start your computer
- **Minimize to Tray**: Keep snap running quietly in the background while you work

### 🛠️ Troubleshooting

**Problem: snap doesn't seem to work**
- Make sure you're in a Google Meet session (on meet.google.com)
- Check that snap is running (look for the icon in your system tray)
- Try restarting snap by right-clicking its icon and selecting "Reload"

**Problem: The keyboard shortcut doesn't respond**
- Open the snap settings and re-assign the hotkey
- Make sure no other program is using the same shortcut

**Problem: I can't find the download file**
- Check your Downloads folder (usually C:\Users\YourName\Downloads)
- If you see a downloaded zip file, extract it first (right-click and select "Extract All")
